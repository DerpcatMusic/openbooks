# OpenBooks audit, November 2026

What OpenBooks is missing against the law, against Sure and against the paid services, for one owner: an Israeli resident on the osek zair track who also owns a US single-member LLC, with income mostly in USD from foreign platforms. Checked against branch `mp-qa2` (the TypeScript monorepo). OpenBooks is a tool, not tax advice; items marked **confirm** need an accountant's ruling.

## 1. Legal and tax findings

Ranked by how much money or legal exposure each carries.

| # | Finding | Exposure | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The 1301 applied osek zair's 30% deemed expenses to someone who also owns a transparent US LLC. The zair track excludes income attributed from a transparent entity and controlling holders of a closely held company. **Confirm** how the Tax Authority treats this LLC. | Whole-year reassessment, loss of zair status | Fixed in this overhaul |
| 2 | Form 150 (holding in a foreign entity) is required with the 1301 every year, but only showed on the "You" card. | Missing form | Fixed |
| 3 | No foreign-currency conversion. Israel requires the Bank of Israel representative rate on each transaction's date; the app used hard-coded 3.06 and 3.6. LLC income never reached the 1301. | Wrong taxable income | Fixed (BoI rates, per-transaction) |
| 4 | Issued receipts could be overwritten, deleted, renumbered or backdated through the API, numbers were assigned in the browser, there was no cancellation document, and "מקור" could be reprinted. | Books not acceptable (also a zair exclusion) | Fixed |
| 5 | Form 5472: unfillable fields were silently left blank (an incomplete 5472 counts as not filed, $25,000); lines 1n/1o hard-coded "United States"; line 43a ticked although a foreign-owned DE must not complete it; Part V saw only equity rows; the pro forma 1120 always used the 2025 blank; line 1c counted cash only. | $25,000 penalty | Fixed |
| 6 | Pension credit at 5% instead of 5.5%; stale 225,600 caps; Bituach Leumi base missing the 52% BL deduction; the 1301 estimate ignored deduction and credit boxes; Delaware tax shown as $300 (it is $400). | Over- or under-payment | Fixed |
| 7 | The period lock didn't cover invoices, bills, payroll, trips, statement re-imports or business-type changes. No audit trail. | Closed years could change silently | Fixed |
| 8 | Still not modelled: §75B controlled foreign company and §75B1 foreign professional company; 1042-S per-form withholding input (one foreign-tax figure per entity and year for now); 1040-NR/ITIN; US sales-tax nexus; registered-agent reminder; Israeli expense rules (car, phone, meals shares) are not re-applied to the LLC's expenses before its profit is attributed. | Varies | Open |
| 9 | When an issued invoice was cancelled, its revenue vanished from the accrual P&L, rewriting its (maybe closed) month. | Wrong past periods | Fixed: the original stays in its month, the credit books in its own |

Unverified and worth a look by an accountant: whether the Tax Authority treats this LLC as a transparent entity for the zair exclusion; that any company in the books is controlled by the person (assumed); the 2026 disability-insurance income cap 413,076 (secondary source); the osek zair short-report date (the code uses 31.12 of the following year, the circular says 31.3).

Values checked and correct for 2025 and 2026: income tax brackets, credit point 2,904, zair/patur ceiling 120,000 / 122,833, VAT 18%, Bituach Leumi and health rates and thresholds, mandatory pension 4.45% / 12.55%, pension deduction 11% on qualifying income 232,800, donations 207 minimum and 35% credit, surtax 3% + 2% above 721,560, 1099-NEC $600 (2025) / $2,000 (2026), allocation-number thresholds.

## 2. Against Sure (sure.am)

Sure (AGPL-3.0, fork of Maybe Finance, Rails + Postgres) is a personal-finance app: net worth, budgets, investments. It has no invoices, double-entry, VAT or tax forms, no Israeli banks and no Hebrew. What it does better, and what this overhaul took from it (ideas, not code):

| Sure has | OpenBooks now |
|---|---|
| Cross-currency transfer matching with rejected pairs | Same entity and across entities (LLC → owner draws) |
| Daily balance table with anchors, net worth in one currency | Daily balances, opening balances, `/api/networth` |
| Cash-flow sankey, spending-vs-normal treemap, money in/out, net worth line | All four, hand-drawn SVG |
| Recurring and subscription detection, rule-based insights | Same |
| SimpleFIN, Wise, Enable Banking connectors | Same, plus Mercury, Plaid and Israeli banks (the three new ones are tested against fake servers only) |
| — | Audit trail of every change (who: app, MCP, AI or sync), History page |

## 3. Against paid services

- **doola**: $1,999/yr for federal and state filing with DIY bookkeeping software; a human bookkeeper only at $2,999/yr. **Firstbase** charges $899/yr for exactly the 5472 + pro forma 1120 OpenBooks fills. Plain 5472 services cost $49–$449.
- Worth paying for: a one-time US opinion that the LLC has no US trade or business or permanent establishment (e.g. Greenback, 5472 $405, 1040-NR from $555), and an Israeli accountant for the LLC's Israeli treatment (item 1).
- Israel: Morning (Green Invoice) is the market standard for documents and bank connections; none of the Israeli products handle USD platform income, per-transaction BoI conversion, Form 150 or a US LLC.

## 4. Bank connections without a licence

- SimpleFIN protocol: SimpleFIN Bridge (US/Canada) and Lunch Flow (30+ countries); the user pays the bridge about $15–35/yr.
- Enable Banking restricted mode for EU/UK: free on your own accounts, under Enable Banking's licence.
- Direct tokens: Mercury, Wise (UK/EEA profiles can't read statements with a personal token), Plaid with your own keys.
- Israel: israeli-bank-scrapers, run locally on your own credentials. A licence under the Financial Information Service Law costs ₪16,000/yr plus capital requirements.
- GoCardless Bank Account Data is closed to new signups; Midday has wound down.

## 5. Structure for more countries

Already in place: one package per country under `packages/countries/<cc>` (see `_template/README.md`), year tables with a source for every number, pure calc functions, form field maps pinned to the blank PDFs. Keep adding countries as packages; keep rates in tables, never in code.
