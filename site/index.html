Tax, built in

The forms fill themselves from your books.

Country packs carry each year's rates, deadlines and forms, with the source for every number.

  • Israel: Form 1301 printed over the official form, a proof pack PDF, and a planner for osek zair's 30% deemed expenses, Bituach Leumi, pension and credit points.
  • US: Form 5472 and pro forma 1120 for a foreign-owned single-member LLC, with filing deadlines and a checklist.
  • Cross-border: one person, both countries. Treaty withholding and what each country taxes, in one place.
  • Taxximizer asks the questions an accountant would and shows what each answer is worth.

Private by design

No servers. Nothing to breach.

  • Your books are one SQLite file. Back up the folder and you've backed up everything.
  • Bank logins and API keys are sealed with a passphrase (AES-256-GCM, scrypt).
  • Listens on 127.0.0.1 only. Blocks other websites and DNS rebinding.
  • Privacy mode scrambles every number for screenshots and screen-shares.
